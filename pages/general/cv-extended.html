Professional Profile

Global macro economist with over a decade of experience building large-scale macroeconomic forecasting pipelines, producing institutional research, and delivering bespoke analytical solutions to buy-side and sell-side clients. Deep expertise in emerging markets — particularly Latin America and Central & Eastern Europe — with a quantitative foundation spanning DSGE modelling, Bayesian time-series, VAR/SVAR, state-space methods, and applied machine learning. Track record of supporting trading desks, portfolio managers, and policy researchers with rigorous, data-driven intelligence under tight deadlines. Published researcher in social policy, poverty measurement, and macroeconomics. Trained at the University of Oxford’s TLC Econometrics Summer School. Spanish (first language), English (fluent).
